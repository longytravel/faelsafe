## What it costs

You told us the shape you want: start with a basic package, then add what earns its keep. That's exactly how we've priced it — with two differences from how software companies usually do it, which we'll get to.

**To start: £3,000, then £200 a month.**

The £3,000 gets the website rebuilt, analytics working, and the lead radar live on real data — the 785 enforcement notices, the care home ratings, refreshed and on your screen. The £200 a month keeps it all running: data feeds refreshing, site hosted, tools maintained, small fixes done without a quote.

You'll recognise those numbers — they're BigChange's 2017 prices, deliberately. That deal worked; you're still with them nine years on. But you also told us what happened next: £200 became £1,200, one 'extra' at a time. So, the two differences:

**First: the whole menu, with prices, is on this page.** Nothing gets revealed later. Each add-on is a one-off price. The only thing that ever changes the monthly is if an add-on needs its own data feed — and that's printed next to it before you say yes.

| Add-on | One-off | Adds to monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Live tender tracking — public-sector fire safety work, straight onto the radar screen | £750 | +£25 |
| Client compliance portal — your clients log in and see their certificates and service due dates | £1,500 | nothing |
| Extra radar data source — a new region or register you want watched | £350 | nothing |
| Standards-change watch — which BS changes affect which of your clients | £950 | +£25 |

One honest note on the last one: it's not for sale yet. We won't take money for it until we've proven it works — you'll see it running first.

**Second: you own everything we build.** Code, data, website — yours outright, no licence, no per-user fee. The £200 isn't rent on the software; it's the running of it. Stop paying and you keep the lot — the feeds just stop refreshing until someone, us or anyone else, switches them back on.

No contract term. Cancel any month, keep everything, owe nothing further.

For scale: the whole menu — everything on this page, running for a full year — comes to about £9,000. That's less than one year of Barbour, and about six weeks of a business development manager once you've paid the salary, the car and the commission.
